Today's Featured Plants

  • American Papaw!

  • Scientific Name: Asimina triloba.

  • Description: A deciduous tree. It has large leaves and produces a large, yellowish-green to brown fruit!

  • Growing Description: Sun. Water. Soil. Grow within the hardiness zones of 5-9! Trees grow within 7-8 years!


  • Common Lady Fern!

  • Scientific Name: Athyrium filix-femina.

  • Description: A fern of 3 common species! They have several different fronds appearing from central points. But there tends to be yellow-green or brown spots on these ferns.

  • Growing Description: Sun. Water. Soil. Damp, shady, woodland environments.


  • Black Choke Berry!

  • Scientific Name: Aronia melanocarpa.

  • Description: A kind of black berry! It's a species of shrub with glossy dark green leaves that take a red color in the fall! This shrub grows up to 1 meter or more!

  • Growing Description: Sun. Water. Soil. Needs some shade but grows well in the sun.


  • American Walking Fern!

  • Scientific Name: Asplenium rhizophyllum.

  • Description: A kind of fern! It has undivided evergreen leaves, and long narrow leaf tips. They also grow in tufts!

  • Growing Description: Sun. Water. Soil. Shaded boulders, bledges, or crevices. Meaning it also means shade in order to grow.


  • Box Elder Maple!

  • Scientific Name: Acer negundo.

  • Description: A kind of Maple Tree that grows up to 10-25m! The shoots ar egreen, with some whitish-violet cax coatings! The bark on the trunk is pale grey or light brown. The Box Maple unlike other maple has pinate compound leaves that have 3-7 leaflets!

  • Growing Description: Sun. Water. Soil. Lots of water, these plants like growing on riverbeds and in wet soil!
