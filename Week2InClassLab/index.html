Pennsylvania Native Plant: Yellow Lotus

Nelumbo lutea

  • Common Name: Yellow Lotus.
  • Scientific Name: Nelumbo lutea

The Yellow Lotus is an aquatic plant that grows in lakes and swamps throughout the United States. Despite it's name being the "yellow" lotus, the flowers of the lotus can range from white to pale yellow. In Pennsylvania it unfortunately is considered endangered, and their populations have been shown to have a low level of genetic diversity.

  • Native Range: Southern Ontario, Iowa, and Minnesota to southern New England and New York; south to Florida, Cuba, Jamaica, and Hispaniola; west to Texas; south through Mexico to Honduras.
  • Sunlight Requirements: Require's direct sunlight.
  • Soil Requirements: Wet soil. At the bottom of ponds or pots.
  • Water Requirements: High amounts of water, due to it being an aquatic plant.
  • Blooming Season: Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.
  • Wildlife benefits: Food source for animals and people.
  • Plant height:6 feet tall, two feet wide.
